A different way to organize knowledge

Beyond documents.
Beyond search.

Files are where knowledge gets stored. Context is what people actually need. ShanText turns scattered information into living, reusable context objects that AI systems can understand and share.

◇

Create

Turn emails, meetings, documents, databases, and professional judgment into named context objects. One document may yield many facts; one object may draw from many sources.

⬡

Share

Compose objects into larger views, give each a stable identity, and determine which people, teams, and AI applications can access them.

↗

Use anywhere

Make relevant context available to authorized assistants through open interfaces such as APIs and MCP, without rebuilding it separately for each AI application.

A different model of ownership

Your context shouldn't belong to your AI application.

Switch assistants, collaborate with colleagues, or use specialized professional software. The underlying context should stay yours: persistent, portable, and accessible under your rules.
