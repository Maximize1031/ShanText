A different way to organize knowledge

Beyond documents.
Beyond search.

Files are where knowledge gets stored. Context is what people actually need. ShanText explores turning scattered information into living, reusable context objects that AI systems can understand and share.

◇

Atomic by design

One document can contain many facts. One fact can depend on many sources. Context objects reflect what information means—not where it lives.

⬡

Composable context

Combine objects into client, matter, engagement, or organization views. Reuse the same context across teams and workflows.

↗

Available across AI

Designed as an independent layer that assistants and professional AI applications could access through common interfaces such as APIs and MCP.

⌁

Kept current

As sources change, derived facts and summaries can be refreshed, reconciled, and versioned—without rebuilding knowledge by hand.

◎

Evidence attached

Know where information came from, when it was true, and what supports it. Answers should be traceable, not merely plausible.

♧

Scoped by permission

Context sharing should respect client boundaries, matter restrictions, and professional confidentiality at every level.
