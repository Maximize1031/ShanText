The problem

Your business keeps learning.
Its knowledge stays scattered.

Important information lives in documents, email, meetings, business systems and individual AI conversations. New answers and work products are created every day, but they rarely become reusable context for everyone else. People and AI agents end up searching, repeating work, or making decisions without the latest information.

▤

Knowledge is fragmented

Existing records and new information are spread across tools, teams, and personal workspaces.

✧

AI work gets trapped

Analyses, answers, code and documents created by AI agents often stay inside individual chats instead of improving shared organizational context.

⌁

Decisions take longer

People spend time reconstructing what happened, finding current facts and resolving conflicting information.

The solution

One continuously updated
source of business context.

ShanText is designed to turn a continuous flow of information into organized, shareable context objects. Each object brings together what is known about a client, project, matter or decision—and evolves as new information arrives.

01 / Bring it together

Capture what matters.

Connect existing knowledge with new meeting notes, documents, business-system updates, AI conversations and agent-generated work.

02 / Keep it current

Update shared context.

Organize information into context objects, preserve its sources, track changes and surface unresolved questions instead of treating every new artifact as another isolated file.

03 / Put it to work

Ask and act faster.

Make relevant, permissioned context quickly available to employees and AI agents when a question, task or decision comes up.

What is a context object?

Everything relevant.
In one usable view.

Imagine a client project. Its context includes the original agreement, the latest meeting notes, decisions made by the team, AI-generated analysis, open questions and next steps. ShanText organizes these into a shared object that stays useful as the project changes.
